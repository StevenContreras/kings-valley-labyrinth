# King's Valley Labyrinth MVP Rules

## Basics
- Two players.
- Abstract Strategy Game.

## Board
- 7x7 board.
- Center valley is row 4, column 4.

## Pieces
- Each player has 1 king and 6 guards.
- All pieces move the same way.
- Only kings can settle on the valley.

## Walls
- Walls are neutral pieces.
- Walls cannot move in the MVP.
- Walls block movement like pieces.
- Pieces cannot pass through walls or land on walls.

## Movement
- A player chooses one piece and one direction.
- Pieces move in 8 queen-like directions orthogonally or diagonally.
- A piece must slide as far as possible in the chosen direction.
- Pieces cannot voluntarily stop early.
- Pieces stop before the board edge, a wall, or another piece.
- All pieces may pass through the valley.
- Guards may not settle on the valley.

## Win Condition
- A player wins by settling their king on the center valley.
- Passing through the valley does not win.
